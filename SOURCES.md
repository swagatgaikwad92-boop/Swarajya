# Historical sources and confidence log

Rule used throughout: **historical text** and **gameplay numbers** are separate.
Every historical entry in `data/*.json` has a `confidence` tag:

- **HIGH**: broadly agreed in the scholarship.
- **MEDIUM**: commonly stated; details or dates vary by account.
- **UNCERTAIN**: disputed, thinly documented, or a gameplay assumption.

## Source pointers (bibliographic, NOT yet verified page by page)
These are real works used as starting points. No page-level citations have been checked.
Before treating any entry as verified, check it against the books below and record the page.

- Gordon, Stewart. *The Marathas 1600-1818* (New Cambridge History of India, 1993).
- Sarkar, Jadunath. *Shivaji and His Times*.
- Mehendale, Gajanan B. *Shivaji: His Life and Times*.
- Eaton, Richard M. *A Social History of the Deccan, 1300-1761* (Cambridge, 2005).

## Known disputes flagged in the data
- Shivaji's birth year (1627 vs 1630).
- Date and manner of Torna's acquisition (c. 1646-47; force or arrangement).
- Whether and when Kondana was held and returned (c. 1649 tradition tied to Shahaji's release).
- Sequence of events around Purandar (c. 1648).
- Chakan's holder in 1646-49; Firangoji Narsala's role then (best documented at Chakan in 1660).
- Pune's status: it was part of a jagir held within the Adil Shahi system, so "player-owned" is a simplification.

## Deliberate gameplay assumptions (not history)
- Start ownership of every territory, garrison sizes, wall values, revenue, manpower.
- All combat, supply, stability and recruitment numbers.
- The "Bijapur Sardar" commander is a **fictional placeholder**.
- The win condition (hold four forts at once) is a game target, not a documented state.
- Turn length (3 months) and unit scale (one unit = a company-sized body) are abstractions.
- No artillery recruitment for the player is a gameplay constraint, not a historical claim.
